A five-minute reset

Make “yes” more deliberate.

Scope creep is rarely one dramatic request. It is a series of tiny ambiguities. Use this pass before you confirm the work.

Keep it visible: send the answers back with the proposal, brief, or next-step email.

What is the one outcome?Describe the finished result in one plain sentence.
What is explicitly not included?Name the boundary while the request is still friendly.
Who decides it is done?Confirm the approver, review rounds, and handoff point.
What changes the estimate?List the inputs or decisions that would require a new quote.
What is the next written step?Turn the conversation into a date, owner, and clear reply.
